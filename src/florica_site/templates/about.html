Our point of view

Better technology work starts with clarity.

Florica Ltd is a general-purpose consultancy with a sharp focus on AI and technology. We help organisations make confident decisions, build useful products, and improve the way work gets done.

Our model is intentionally direct: experienced practitioners, small teams, clear outcomes. We work across strategy, design, engineering, and change because valuable technology rarely fits inside one discipline.

We are optimistic about AI and rigorous about how it is applied. The strongest solutions respect the people who use them, the information they depend on, and the business that must operate them after launch.
